Palmshade Heat is an original top-down 2D open-world crime game set in the sun-soaked coastal Palmshade City (3600x2700): you play Dante Marlowe, back home broke, taking story work from Rosa Delgado at the Palms Diner across five missions — meeting Rosa, stealing your first wheels, running a hot package (which earns you a pistol), a timed shore run, and a final heat wave escape to your safehouse — while exploring on foot and by car, stealing cars, dodging traffic and pedestrians, outrunning a wanted-star police system, throwing punches, working taxi side-jobs, clearing heat at Turbo Detail, and saving at the safehouse, all with a HUD, minimap, title/pause/busted/wasted screens, and synthesized WebAudio sound with no audio files, no libraries, and no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts, then open http://localhost:8080 (the game itself is static: game/index.html)
```
